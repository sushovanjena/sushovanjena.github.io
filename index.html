Post-training · vision-language · accessibility

Smol-VL-BLV — Reinforcement based post-training alignment for blind and low-vision assistance

A compact 500M-parameter vision-language model for blind and low-vision users, built on two post-training stages: teacher-student distillation, then Group Relative Policy Optimisation with a composite reward for directional language, metric distance, and hazard detection. A phase-switched fine-tuning strategy that restores general descriptive quality, countering the catastrophic forgetting that multi-stage post-training tends to introduce.

Fine-tuned with LoRA and RL-based alignment (DPO, GRPO), the model improved spatial reasoning on video descriptions by 101.5% on OCRBench and 44.2% on TextVQA, benchmarked against Qwen2.5-VL 3B and SmolVLM 2.2B. LoRA/RL training scaled across multi-GPU setups, moving from DDP to FSDP to clear out-of-memory errors from larger batches and longer video context windows.

For on-device deployment, a mixed-precision quantization pipeline applied importance-weighted 4-bit quantization (IQ4_NL) across the network while preserving self-attention projections at 5-bit precision — keeping the positional and directional reasoning that visual-to-text token mapping depends on, while more than doubling generation speed.

model compression · anomaly detection

Attend, Distill, Detect: Attention-aware entropy distillation introducing DCAM — a plug and play module between Teacher and Student to make the distillation process more class-invariant and efficient

DCAM (Distributed Convolutional Attention Module) distributes spatial and channel attention across student feature maps for knowledge distillation, paired with multi-scale feature matching and a cosine-distance plus KL-divergence distillation loss. The combination mitigates cross-class interference — a common problem where there is a good variation in morphology of objects and classes, so the feature maps become more hybrid — and improved detection performance by 3.92%.

Teacher-Student architectures · Edge deployment · Quantization

Unified anomaly detection via Knowledge Distillation + Quantization (Post-training and Quantization-aware Training)

The objective is to perform Unsupervised Anomaly Detection when there is only Normal or Non-anomalous images present for Training and during inference, the model is expected to precisely segment the anomalous region in the image. Multi-class training across three knowledge-distillation architectures — Uninformed Students where a 5-layer CNN acts as the Teacher and Student with MSE as matching loss, Reverse-Distillation where there a ResNet-18 based Teacher encoder and Student decoder with heirarchical and intermediate feature matching, and Student-Teacher Feature Pyramid Matching where there is a ResNet-18 Teacher and ResNet-18 Student with intermediate feature matching — combined with post-training quantization and quantization-aware training in PyTorch and TensorRT, deployed on a Jetson NX for real-time industrial inspection. This involves comprehensive experimentation exploring some better Calibration techniques involved in the process of Quantization showimg that Random Normal Calibration perfroms better than Training Data calibration
